Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yula Aria Publisher ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when an authorized creator or team member uses our publishing workspace.

Information we process

Depending on the services you connect and use, we may process: account identifiers and authorization tokens supplied by a connected platform; profile information the platform makes available; media and post information you submit, such as files, captions, titles, visibility choices, and scheduled times; and technical records needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use this information only to provide and maintain the publishing workflow, including connecting accounts at your request, preparing and publishing or scheduling content you approve, preventing duplicate actions, resolving errors, and meeting legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use connected-account information for advertising.

Platform data and sharing

When you connect a third-party platform, we use the permissions you authorize to perform the requested publishing functions. We share the content and publishing settings you choose with that platform solely to carry out your instruction. We do not share your information with unrelated third parties except where required by law or needed to protect the service, users, or others.

Storage and retention

We retain account-connection information, publishing records, and content only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain reliable scheduling and audit records, comply with legal duties, or resolve disputes. Authorization tokens are stored using reasonable security measures and are revoked or removed when they are no longer needed or when you disconnect the account, subject to any required retention period.

Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect a connected account and request deletion of account-connection information and related service data. Visit our Data Deletion page for the request process. Deleting data from our service does not delete content that has already been published on a third-party platform; manage that content directly on the relevant platform.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please protect your account credentials and notify us through the support channel below if you believe your account has been compromised.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date when changes take effect.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open a private support request through the Yula Aria Publisher support channel. Please do not post account tokens, government IDs, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
