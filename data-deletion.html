Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Yula Aria Publisher account-connection data, access tokens, saved publishing records, and stored content that is eligible for deletion.

How to request deletion

  1. Open a request through the Yula Aria Publisher support channel.
  2. State that you are requesting data deletion and identify the connected platform account only by its public username or other non-sensitive identifier.
  3. We may request additional verification through a private channel before acting on a request.

Do not include access tokens, passwords, identity documents, or private account information in a public issue.

What happens next

After verification, we will delete eligible data within 30 days unless we must retain specific information for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons. We will confirm completion through the support channel.

Important

Deleting data from Yula Aria Publisher does not remove posts already made on TikTok or any other connected platform. To remove published content, use the tools provided by that platform.
